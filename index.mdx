## Understand the essentials

- **Business dashboard:** the workspace where you manage a selected business.
- **Bryng Store:** where customers discover businesses and products.
- **Payments:** your business’s financial history. Customers find their purchases in **Orders**.
- **Join:** free following of a business. Joining does not grant access to paid products, and leaving does not revoke purchases.

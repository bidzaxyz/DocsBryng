## Create a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Products">
    Select your business in the dashboard, open **Products**, and choose **Create product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add details and pricing">
    Enter a name, headline, description, and product images. Choose free or paid access and configure the pricing shown in the editor. Review your product URL before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Save the product, then use its **Actions** menu to open the public page or copy its URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Changing a saved product URL replaces the previous address. Update any links you have already shared.
</Warning>

## Submit to Bryng Store

Saving a product and submitting it for Store review are separate actions.
Choose **Submit to Bryng Store for review** from the product’s **Actions** menu.
The submission checklist requires a business logo, product name, headline, description, and product image.

A submission remains pending until reviewed. If it is declined, read the reason, make the necessary changes, and submit it again.
Acknowledging the reason does not approve the product.

## Control visibility

Hiding a product removes it from listings while keeping its direct link accessible. Hiding does not make the product private.

## Contact Bryng

Email [help@bryng.xyz](mailto:help@bryng.xyz). Include the business name, the page or task involved, what happened, and an order reference if relevant.
For developer questions, describe the integration you want to build.

<Warning>
  Do not include passwords, sign-in codes, API keys, merchant payment secrets, or full card details. Redact private customer data from screenshots.
</Warning>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does joining a business unlock its paid products?">
    No. Join is free following. Product access comes from acquiring the relevant product. Leaving does not revoke purchases.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my product not in Bryng Store?">
    Saving a product does not submit it for review. Check its review status in **Products** and follow the [Store submission guide](/portal/products).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are my website changes not public?">
    Edits update your private draft. Publish the saved changes explicitly, and check the domain status. See [Websites and publishing](/portal/websites).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do customers find their purchases?">
    Customers use **Orders** in their account. Business managers use **Payments** for merchant financial history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create an API key or webhook?">
    API key and webhook creation are not available yet. The [developer overview](/developers/overview) explains what documentation is coming.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

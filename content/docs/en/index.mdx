## Understand the essentials

- **Business dashboard:** where you manage the selected business.
- **Bryng Store:** where customers discover businesses and products.
- **Payments:** your business’s financial history. Customers find their purchases in **Orders**.
- **Join:** free following. It does not grant paid product access, and leaving does not revoke purchases.

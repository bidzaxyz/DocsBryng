<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [Bryng](https://bryng.xyz/home), enter your email, and use the sign-in code sent to your inbox. Complete account setup and accept the required terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select or create a business">
    Use the profile switcher to select a business you manage, or choose **Start a business**.
    Enter your business name using Latin letters, numbers, and punctuation, then select a category and revenue range. Website and phone details are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it yours">
    Open **Business settings** to update your details and images. Bryng generates your business address automatically; changing the business name does not change that address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find your next task

| Dashboard section | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Products | Create products and manage their pricing and visibility. |
| Payments | Review your business’s financial history and payment details. |
| Customers | View customer records for the selected business. |
| Websites | Edit website drafts and publish saved changes. |
| Business settings | Manage business details, images, and payment settings. |
| Developer | Find developer tools for the selected business. |

## Work with the right business

Check the selected business before editing. Switching profiles changes your workspace, not your permissions.
Owners and admins manage day-to-day business activity; some ownership and payment-provider settings are reserved for the owner.

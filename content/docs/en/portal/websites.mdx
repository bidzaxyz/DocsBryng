---
title: "Websites and publishing"
description: "Edit a website draft and publish your saved changes."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a design">
    Open **Websites** in the selected business and choose **Create Website**.
    Select Product Showcase, Clothing Store, General Store, or Book Store, then enter a website name and language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the draft">
    Click text on the preview to edit it. Replace images, select products, and update your branding using the editor controls. Each design uses a fixed composition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Review your saved draft and domain status before publishing. Draft edits do not change the public website until you explicitly publish them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Draft and live website

Your draft and published website are separate. Review the saved version before publishing, and check the public page afterward.

## Domains

A domain must pass ownership, DNS, and TLS checks before activation. Entering a domain name alone does not connect it.
Follow the records and verification instructions displayed for that website.

## Write your own policies

Terms, Privacy, and Sales & refunds pages begin empty. Add policies that reflect how your business actually operates.
Bryng’s platform policies are separate from your merchant policies.

## Products on your website

Choose which products appear using the editor’s product controls. Product details and pricing are managed in [Products](/portal/products).

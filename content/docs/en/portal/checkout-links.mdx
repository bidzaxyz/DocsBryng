---
title: "Checkout links"
description: "Create a shareable checkout for a free or one-time purchase."
---

A checkout link sends a customer directly to a purchase. You can use an existing business product or create a new one while making the link.

## Create a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment tools">
    Select your business and open **Payments → Accept a Payment**. If you see **Activate payments**, ask the owner to complete the payment connection in **Business settings → Payments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a product and price">
    Select an existing product or add a new product’s name and URL. Add a checkout description, then choose **Free** or **One-time** pricing.
    Review the price carefully: a checkout link’s price can differ from the product’s price without changing that product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and share">
    Check the preview and save the link. Find saved links under **More → Checkout Links**, then copy or open the link to share it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Recurring checkout links — coming soon.** The recurring option in the link editor is unavailable. For recurring product pricing, see [Subscriptions](/portal/subscriptions).
</Info>

## What happens after sharing?

Opening a link does not create an order. When the customer submits checkout, Bryng creates a saved attempt and shows its verified result. Find the attempt in **Payments**; the customer can find it in **Orders** after signing in with the checkout email.

A new product created through the link editor starts hidden from listings. Hidden products can still be reached through their direct link; hidden does not mean private.

## Deactivate a link

Open **Checkout Links** and deactivate a saved link to stop future use. This does not cancel or refund an existing order. An already-created payment attempt retains its own status and recovery flow.

See [Payments and orders](/portal/payments) for pending or unsuccessful results.

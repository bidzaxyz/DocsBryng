## Set recurring pricing

In a product’s pricing settings, choose recurring pricing and review the amount and billing interval. Supported intervals include weekly, monthly, yearly, and a custom interval shown in the editor.

A subscription checkout contains one product and one unit. The customer must explicitly agree to recurring billing before continuing.

<Note>
  Pricing is locked while the product has pending, active, past-due, or cancelled-but-still-paid-through subscribers. The agreed subscription price does not change with later product edits.
</Note>

## Customer management

Customers can open **Settings → Payment Methods** to find their subscriptions and saved payment methods, grouped by business. A subscription shows its status, amount, billing period, next attempt, and paid-through date when available.

Choose **Manage** to change the subscription’s card through secure provider verification or confirm cancellation. A saved card for one business is not a shared card for every business on Bryng.

## Cancellation and access

Cancelling stops future renewals; already-paid access remains until its end date. Cancellation is separate from a refund. Leaving a business’s free following is also separate from cancelling a subscription.

## Pending or failed payments

Use the saved subscription and payment status. A browser return does not confirm payment, and an unresolved payment must not be treated as a failure. Check the next attempt shown and [contact support](/support) if you need help.

Business owners cannot disconnect or switch the merchant account while subscriptions remain live. This keeps existing billing attached to the correct business payment connection.
